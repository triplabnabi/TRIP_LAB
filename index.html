Capsaicin & Gut Microbiome Research

We were the first lab to report increased Akkermansia muciniphila with capsaicin administration, linking it to powerful anti-obesity effects.

Prebiotic Innovation

Our research established sweet prebiotics like isomaltooligosaccharides (IMO) as superior alternatives that improve metabolic health by boosting good bacteria.

TRP Channel Pharmacology

Our work delves into how cooling compounds interact with TRP channels to produce therapeutic sensory effects, opening new possibilities for pain and inflammation treatment.

Meet Our Research Team

Dr. Mahendra Bishnoi

Scientist F | Diet/Nutrition & Receptor Pharmacology

Dr. Bishnoi leads the lab with a focus on providing rigorous scientific mechanisms to ancient beliefs about the health benefits of dietary constituents. His research utilizes nutrigenomics and receptor-based approaches to promote 'food as medicine' and address global nutritional challenges. Before joining in 2011, he completed postdoctoral fellowships in pain and diabetes research at Southern Illinois University and in nicotine addiction at the University of Florida's McKnight Brain Institute.
