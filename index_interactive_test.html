Promoting Food as Medicine

Our objective is to provide scientific mechanisms to ancient beliefs about the health benefits of dietary constituents.

Explore Our Discoveries

Contribution to Scientific Advancement

Our lab provides molecular proofs for the health benefits of food constituents like capsaicin, cinnamaldehyde, and menthol, blending ancient wisdom with modern science.

Capsaicin

We were the first to report that capsaicin increases Akkermansia muciniphila, linking it to a powerful anti-obesity effect.

Gut Microbiome

We established that sweet prebiotics like IMO improve metabolic health by boosting good bacteria.

Menthol

Our research delves into how cooling compounds like menthol interact with TRP channels to produce their signature sensory effects.
