## 🌟 About Us

The TR(i)P Lab (Translational Research in TRP Channels) pioneers molecular understanding of dietary constituents through advanced TRP channel research. We blend ancient wisdom with modern science, combining traditional knowledge with cutting-edge computational and experimental approaches.

### Our Mission
- Provide **scientific mechanisms** for ancient dietary health beliefs
- Advance molecular research in **thermal sensitivity and TRP channels**
- Explore **nutrition-pharmacology interfaces**
- Promote **food as medicine** paradigm through rigorous research

## 🧬 Current Research Focus

### TRP Channel Pharmacology
- **TRPV1 Channel Research**: First identified temperature-sensitive TRP channel
- **Thermal Sensors**: Molecular mechanisms of heat and chemical sensing
- **Signal Transduction**: Advanced ion channel research techniques

### Nutrigenomics & Metabolism
- **Food as Medicine**: Dietary constituents' impact on metabolic pathways
- **Capsaicin Pharmacology**: Anti-obesity effects and therapeutic applications
- **Gut Microbiome Interactions**: Prebiotic research and microbial health

### Breakthrough Discoveries
- **Akkermansia muciniphila Link**: First lab to demonstrate capsaicin's microbiome effects
- **Sweet Prebiotics**: Established IMO as superior metabolic health alternative
- **TRP Channel Cooling**: Molecular mechanisms of menthol and related compounds
