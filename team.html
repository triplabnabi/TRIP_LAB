Our Team

Dr. Mahendra Bishnoi

Scientist F | Diet/Nutrition & Receptor Pharmacology

Dr. Bishnoi leads the lab with a focus on providing rigorous scientific mechanisms to ancient beliefs about the health benefits of dietary constituents. His research utilizes nutrigenomics and receptor-based approaches to promote 'food as medicine' and address global nutritional challenges. Before joining in 2011, he completed postdoctoral fellowships in pain and diabetes research at Southern Illinois University and in nicotine addiction at the University of Florida's McKnight Brain Institute.

Postdoctoral Fellows

Dr. Anna Rodriguez

Postdoctoral Fellow

Dr. Rodriguez is a computational biologist developing molecular dynamics simulations for TRP channel drug discovery. Her research focuses on understanding the molecular mechanisms of TRP channel activation and modulation through advanced computational approaches.

Dr. James Liu

Postdoctoral Fellow

Dr. Liu is an electrophysiology expert specializing in patch-clamp techniques and TRP channel biophysics. He leads our electrophysiology research program, focusing on single-channel recordings and ion permeation mechanisms.

MJ

M. Jones

Postdoctoral Fellow

Dr. Jones focuses on metabolic research and gut microbiome studies related to obesity treatments. His work integrates microbiome analysis with metabolic phenotype characterization to develop novel therapeutic approaches for metabolic disorders.

PhD Students

Michael Kim

5th Year PhD Student

Michael is investigating TRPM8 pharmacology and developing small molecule modulators for pain management. His research aims to understand the molecular basis of TRPM8 channel function and develop novel therapeutic compounds for chronic pain conditions.

A. Das

3rd Year PhD Student

A. Das is studying prebiotic compounds and their effects on gut microbiota composition. Her work focuses on how dietary prebiotics modulate the gut microbiome to improve metabolic health and prevent metabolic disorders.
