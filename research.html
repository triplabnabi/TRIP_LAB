Our Research

We focus on providing molecular proofs for the health benefits of different food and food constituents, blending ancient wisdom with modern science.

Capsaicin & The Gut Microbiome

It is noteworthy that we have been first laboratory to report increase in Akkermansia muciniphila on capsaicin administration and its possible role in its anti-obesity effect. This groundbreaking work links a common dietary component to significant metabolic health outcomes through its interaction with the gut microbiome.

Metabolic Health & Prebiotics

While many artificial sweeteners can cause metabolic complications through gut dysbiosis, our lab has established that sweet prebiotics like isomaltooligosaccharides (IMO) have profound gut benefits. We found they improve metabolic health by increasing good bacterial abundance and the production of short chain fatty acids, making them a viable alternative for developing novel healthy foods.

TRP Channels & Sensory Compounds

Our research delves into how cooling compounds like menthol interact with TRP (Transient Receptor Potential) channels to produce their signature sensory effects. Understanding these molecular interactions is key to developing new therapeutic agents for pain and inflammation.
